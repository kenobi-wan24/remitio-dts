FILE UPLOAD LIMIT (important!)
 PHP's default upload limit is often 2 MB. Our app allows 10 MB per file.
 Find your php.ini:   php --ini
 Set:   upload_max_filesize = 12M
        post_max_size = 64M
 Save, then stop and re-run `php artisan serve`.
